Renovating a bathroom in Hamilton

Hamilton is an inner suburb known for Beaumont Street, with Victorian and Federation cottages on narrow, flat blocks.

Bathrooms in older Hamilton homes are often small and were built before current waterproofing standards. Asbestos-containing sheeting was common in older wet areas and must be removed safely.

Narrow cottages can mean tight access for removing old tiles and bringing in materials, and a small room needs careful design to fit a good shower.

In heritage areas, internal bathroom work usually isn't affected, but any change to the outside of the house may need planning approval.

What to mention on your quote request

Tell us the suburb (Hamilton), which bathroom (main, ensuite or powder room), what you'd like to change and any timing needs. We pass that to one local bathroom renovator who services Hamilton, and they contact you directly. The request is free, and there's no obligation to go ahead.

Rules and approvals

Hamilton is in the City of Newcastle area. In NSW, residential work over $5,000 needs a licensed contractor and a written contract, and work over $20,000 needs Home Building Compensation cover before any deposit. See our Renovation process guide.
