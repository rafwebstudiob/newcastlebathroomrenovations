Bathroom renovation costs in Newcastle

In short: We don't publish our own prices. A national guide says most bathroom renovations cost about A$8,000–35,000, but a local renovator has to quote your bathroom.

Use published ranges only as a starting point, and allow a buffer.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

We don't set or advertise our own prices. The figures below are from a published national guide, with a link. They are not quotes for Newcastle. A local renovator has to price your bathroom.

Published cost ranges

  • hipages' bathroom renovation cost guide (updated January 2026) cites Housing Industry Association data showing the average Australian bathroom renovation cost A$26,000 in 2023, and says a renovation can cost anywhere from about A$8,000 to A$35,000.
  • It breaks this into budget (A$8,000–15,000), standard (A$15,000–35,000) and premium (A$35,000 or more) projects, and says per-square-metre quotes of about A$2,300–4,600 are common.
  • It suggests a buffer of 10–20% for unexpected costs.

What usually changes the quote

Moving things

Moving the toilet, shower or vanity means moving plumbing and drainage, which adds time and cost.

Waterproofing and tiling

Waterproofing is a critical step, and tiling is often the biggest labour item. See our waterproofing guide.

Fixtures and fittings

Bath, shower screen, vanity, toilet, tapware, heating and lighting.

The house

The housing mix runs from Victorian terraces and workers' cottages in Cooks Hill, Hamilton and Mayfield, through post-war brick and fibro homes in the western suburbs, to newer estates around Fletcher and Minmi. Older bathrooms can hide rot, old waterproofing failures or asbestos-containing sheeting, which add cost.

About the budget field on our form

The budget bands are for you to show a range if you have one. They are not a price list.
