Renovating a bathroom in Newcastle

Newcastle's older inner suburbs have plenty of bathrooms in need of renovation: tiny bathrooms in Victorian and Federation cottages in Cooks Hill and Hamilton, and original post-war bathrooms in Waratah, Adamstown and New Lambton. Older homes can hide asbestos-containing sheeting and failed waterproofing.

In NSW, waterproofing is specialist work that must be done by, or subcontracted to, a licensed waterproofer, and residential work over $5,000 needs a licensed contractor and a written contract.

How much does a bathroom renovation cost in Newcastle?

It depends on size, layout changes, waterproofing, tiling and fittings. We don't publish our own prices. Our bathroom cost guide links to published national ranges.

Does the waterproofer need a licence?

In NSW, waterproofing is specialist work. A renovator must subcontract it to a contractor licensed for waterproofing. See our waterproofing guide.

What licence should the renovator have?

In NSW, residential work over $5,000 needs a licensed contractor and a written contract, and work over $20,000 needs Home Building Compensation cover before any deposit.
